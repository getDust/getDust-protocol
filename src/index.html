    Mechanics

    One offer, four moves, exactly one ending

    Custody stays inside vault code through every move. Both closing moves can be tried; only one succeeds while its rival bounces.

    1. 01

      Freeze

      Choose a batch of one token. Our vault program takes custody, stamped with exit level, window length and desired payment. Nobody bit yet? Withdraw whenever you wish.

    2. 02

      Match

      Someone pays your asking payment. Nearly all arrives instantly, inside one transaction; a thin slice feeds service costs. Terms then freeze for everybody.

    3. 03

      Take

      Before the window shuts, your counterparty may settle the exit level and walk away holding your batch. Payment stays with you, plus that exit amount, minus a small cut.

    4. 04

      Lapse

      Window elapsed, batch unclaimed? Any wallet can trigger the return, and everything flows back. Claiming and returning compete; whichever confirms earliest wins.
