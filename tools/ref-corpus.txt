How a contract runs One contract account, from lock to a single settlement.
Lock create_offer Your tokens move into a vault owned by the program, with the strike, the expiry and the premium you ask written into the contract account. Until someone fills it, cancel_unfilled hands the tokens back.
Seller wallet Buyer wallet Contract vault Service vault
Fill buy_option The buyer pays the premium. 97% lands in your wallet in the same transaction and 3% goes to the service vault. From this moment the terms are fixed and the collateral cannot be withdrawn.
Exercise exercise Any time before expiry the buyer can pay the strike in SOL. You receive it minus 0.1%, and the vault releases the tokens to the buyer.
Or expire expire At expiry anyone can call expire and the vault returns the tokens to you. Exercise and expire race for one settlement: the first to land closes the contract, the other is rejected.
